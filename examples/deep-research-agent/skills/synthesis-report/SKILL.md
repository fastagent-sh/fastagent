---
name: synthesis-report
description: Structure multi-source technical research into a cited executive brief without filler.
---

Structure every report with:

1. **Executive Summary** (3 bullets max: what changed and why it matters).
2. **Release Radar** (table: Project, Version, Date, Key Change, Link).
3. **Community Signal** (top Hacker News threads with points, comment count, main debate, and URL).
4. **Open Questions & Risks** (concrete gaps or trade-offs observed across sources).
